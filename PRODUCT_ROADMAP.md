# SAI Books – Product Roadmap & Future Milestones
## Tours & Travels Notes & Accounts System

### Version 1.0.0 (Production-Ready Release - Current)
- [x] **Card-First Notes Architecture**: Replaced rigid tables with human note cards.
- [x] **Zero Accounting Jargon**: Replaced Debits/Credits with Money Received, Money Spent, and Dues.
- [x] **Multi-View System**: Note Cards Grid, Timeline, Status Board (Kanban), Calendar, and Table.
- [x] **Gemini 3.8 Flash NLP Quick-Add**: Sentence parser supporting English, Tamil, and Tanglish.
- [x] **Precision Financial Math**: Exact decimal precision via `Decimal.js` and Indian numbering formatting (`₹1,00,000`).
- [x] **Month-End Closing Wizard**: 4-step closing checklist, anomaly checks, immutable period locking, and Owner-only unlock.
- [x] **Excel & CSV Multi-Report Engine**: Day Book, Profit & Loss, Customer Dues, and GST Summary.
- [x] **Excel Importer**: Drag-and-drop file upload, column mapper preview, validation, and one-click undo.
- [x] **Enterprise Security**: Salted Bcrypt, JWT HTTP-Only cookies, mandatory first-login password update, AES-256 field encryption for Passports and Aadhaar, server-side RBAC, and tamper-proof audit logging.
- [x] **Official Brand Integration**: Sai Tours & Travels official logo integrated across headers, login, and reporting.

---

### Milestone 1.1 (Q4 2026): Automated Messaging & Payments
- [ ] **WhatsApp Business Cloud API Integration**: Send automated payment reminder notifications directly to customer WhatsApp with a single click.
- [ ] **UPI Payment Links & Dynamic QR Codes**: Auto-generate Razorpay/Cashfree dynamic UPI QR codes printed on invoices and sent in WhatsApp messages.
- [ ] **Push Notifications**: Browser and mobile web push reminders for flight departures and passport appointment dates.

---

### Milestone 1.2 (Q1 2027): Direct Bank Statement Reconciliation
- [ ] **ICICI / HDFC / SBI Bank Statement Parser**: Drag-and-drop PDF/CSV bank statements and auto-reconcile against Note Cards.
- [ ] **Account Aggregator (AA) Integration**: Real-time read-only sync of bank transactions for automatic deposit matching.

---

### Milestone 1.3 (Q2 2027): GDS & Airline Direct PNR Sync
- [ ] **Amadeus / Galileo / Indigo Corporate Portal Parser**: Copy-paste or auto-import airline ticket issuance emails to generate Income, Supplier Cost, and Commission cards instantly.
- [ ] **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) Status Webhook**: Live tracking of passport dispatch and police verification updates.

---

### Milestone 2.0 (Q3 2027): Multi-Branch & B2B Partner Portal
- [ ] **Multi-Branch Hierarchy**: Centralized dashboard for Madurai, Trichy, and Chennai branches with inter-branch transfer tracking.
- [ ] **B2B Sub-Agent Portal**: Allow partner travel agents to log bookings under their agency credit limits.
